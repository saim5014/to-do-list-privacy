Privacy Policy

To-Do List App

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To-Do List App is a local task management application. The app allows users to create and manage tasks on their device.

The app may store information such as:

  • User account information, if provided within the app
  • Task titles
  • Task descriptions
  • Task due dates
  • Task completion status

2. Local Data Storage

All information created or entered in the app is stored locally on the user's device using a local SQLite database.

The app does not upload task data to our servers or store task data in a remote database.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or task data with third parties.

4. Internet Usage

The core functionality of the app does not require an internet connection. Tasks and other locally stored information are managed directly on the user's device.

5. Data Security

Because your data is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete tasks from within the application. Uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored application data, depending on the device and operating system.

7. Children's Privacy

To-Do List App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through online services because the app does not transmit user data to a remote server.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play listing.
